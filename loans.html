Understanding Our Loan Policy

Pinnacle Investors promotes responsible borrowing and mutual financial support among its members. All borrowing arrangements are subject to the group's approved rules and applicable procedures.

75%

Standard Borrowing Limit

A member may borrow up to 75% of their personal shares held in the group, subject to the applicable approval process.

4%

Service Charge

A service charge of 4% of the amount borrowed is deducted when the loan is issued. The full principal borrowed, together with applicable interest, remains repayable.

3 Months

Loans Below KSh 50,000

These loans have a maximum repayment period of three months under the existing group provision.

5 Months

Loans of KSh 50,000 and Above

These loans have a maximum repayment period of five months under the existing group provision.

Important: Interest Rate Clarification

The group's existing rules contain a general provision of 6% interest per month and another provision specifying 5% per month on the amount due for loans of KSh 50,000 and above. These provisions are both retained pending clarification by the group's leadership.

Members should confirm the applicable rate and repayment calculation before accepting a loan.

Borrowing Beyond 75% of Personal Shares

A member may request an amount exceeding 75% of their personal shares when supported by a suitable member guarantor whose shares can meet the borrower's financial need, subject to group approval.


Interest Distribution for Guaranteed Loans

  • 25% goes to the guarantor's shares.
  • 25% goes to the borrower's shares.
  • 50% is shared among all members equally.

The guaranteed borrowing provision specifies interest of 6% per month on the amount due.

Loan Repayment & Responsibilities

Members are expected to understand their repayment obligations and honour agreed repayment arrangements.

01. Repayment Period

Loans below KSh 50,000 have a maximum period of three months, while loans of KSh 50,000 and above have a maximum period of five months.

02. Interest Obligations

Applicable interest is determined according to the relevant group borrowing provision. The ordinary-loan interest provisions are pending leadership clarification.

03. Recovery of Outstanding Loans

Under the existing rules, any amount outstanding at the end of the applicable repayment period may be recovered from the borrower's shares.

04. Member Accountability

Every borrower is responsible for meeting the agreed repayment obligations and communicating with the group's leadership where difficulties arise.
