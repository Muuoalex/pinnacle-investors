A Registered Community Group

Pinnacle Investors Self-Help Group is a registered community group in Kenya. The group operates under the name Pinnacle Investors Self Help Group and was registered in accordance with the Community Groups Registration Act, No. 30 of 2022.

We are committed to promoting integrity, transparency, accountability and responsible leadership in our group activities.
