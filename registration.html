Become a Member

Welcome to Pinnacle Investors Self-Help Group! We bring people together through unity, financial discipline, savings and collective investment opportunities.

If you are interested in joining our group, kindly review the membership requirements below.

Membership Requirements

  1. Provide a copy of your National ID or a valid National ID number.
  2. Pay a non-refundable registration fee of KSh 250.
  3. Complete the official Membership Registration Form.
  4. Provide an active mobile phone number.
  5. Provide a valid email address (optional but recommended).
  6. Read, understand and agree to abide by the Pinnacle Investors Self-Help Group Constitution.
Registration Fee: KSh 250 (Non-refundable)

Declaration

By submitting a membership application, you confirm that you have read, understood and agree to comply with the Constitution, rules, regulations and applicable policies of Pinnacle Investors Self-Help Group.

Membership is subject to the group's applicable admission procedures and approval. Payment of the registration fee alone does not automatically confirm membership.

Benefits of Membership

  • Access to group savings and financial discipline initiatives.
  • Opportunities to participate in collective investment initiatives.
  • Access to member financial support arrangements, subject to group rules and approval.
  • Participation in group meetings and decision-making processes.
  • Opportunities for financial learning, networking and personal growth.
  • Participation in group activities and development initiatives.
