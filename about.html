About Pinnacle Investors

A youth-driven Self-Help Group founded on unity, saving, investment and collective growth. Together We Build Hope.

Who We Are

Pinnacle Investors is a youth-driven Self-Help Group founded on 20th May 2024, built on the principles of saving, investment, unity and growth.

It was established to create a strong financial foundation for its members. The group provides a platform for nurturing responsible financial habits, exploring investment opportunities and supporting collective development.

We believe that meaningful progress is achieved when individuals come together with a shared vision, commitment and accountability.

Our Journey

Every great journey begins with a vision. Ours began with a shared desire to build a stronger financial future.

The Beginning of Pinnacle Investors

Pinnacle Investors was founded on 20th May 2024 with a vision of bringing members together through saving, investment and collective financial empowerment.
